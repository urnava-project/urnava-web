The URNAVA Treatise

On Meaning, Existence, and Purpose

Version 2.5  ·  July 7, 2026  ·  202 pp.

An account of meaning, existence, and purpose. It asks how a self composes meaning through its relation to a world and to perspectives not its own, what makes a being remain itself through transformation, and what can orient a will from within when external constraint no longer can. Each question is pursued on its own terms. Together, their answers reveal Self-Origin Ethics.

When Is Using a Perspective Attributed to Another Also Responding to It?

An Event-Level Distinction in Social Cognition

Distinguishes using a perspective attributed to another as a resource from responding to it as theirs. A constructed contrast shows that these roles can coexist, while using a perspective does not by itself establish a response.

A Lower-Bearer Count Is Not a Total Census

The Exactness Burden for Multi-Agent AI

Counting every individual agent need not count every relevant bearer in a multi-agent AI system. The paper identifies the justification required to treat a complete lower-level census as an exact total, including grounds for excluding collective bearers.

Self-Transformation or Generation?

Formation Routes and Organizational Persistence

Complete replacement of an individual’s components need not replace the individual itself. The paper distinguishes self-transformation from generation by whether new components enter an existing maintenance network and carry its reorganization or a successor first forms its own.

Research area: Philosophy of Biology

The Resumption Gap

Assisted Resuscitation and the Bearer of Restored Function

Restoring a function does not by itself establish that its earlier bearer has recovered. The paper distinguishes functional reproducibility from recovery of the same bearer by examining how the later organization was formed.

Research area: Philosophy of Medicine

Purpose

When Differences Matter

Individuating Evaluative Transformation Types

Evaluative changes should be compared by how they reorganize decision-making, not merely by labels or outcomes. The paper distinguishes transformation types through the roles considerations play before and after change: excluding options, permitting trade-offs, and reopening deliberation.

Research area: Philosophy of Action

Beyond Individual Formation

Participatory Reachability and the Public Duty of Education

Education’s public purpose includes developing individual capacities and enabling people to examine the direction of their shared life together. The paper argues that fair access to this inquiry remains an educational responsibility even when individual formation is complete.

Genuine Judgment and the Conditions of Public Reconsideration
